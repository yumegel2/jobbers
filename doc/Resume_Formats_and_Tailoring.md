# Résumé Formats and Tailoring for AI Roles

Use a **single-column, reverse-chronological résumé** for most AI engineering applications. List your most recent experience first. If you’re moving into AI, use a **hybrid résumé** with a short Technical Skills or Selected Projects section above your dated work history. [CareerOneStop résumé formatting guide](https://cloudfront.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Resumes/ResumeGuide/formatting.aspx)

## Format for recruiters and application systems

- Use standard headings such as **Experience**, **Skills**, **Projects**, and **Education**. Put contact information at the top of the document body. Tables, columns, graphics, and text boxes can cause parsing problems. [Greenhouse résumé parsing guidance](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse)
- Use one page if your strongest evidence fits comfortably. Use two when relevant experience warrants it. Follow the employer’s file instructions. If none are given, send a PDF with selectable text and keep a DOCX copy. [CareerOneStop résumé formatting guide](https://cloudfront.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Resumes/ResumeGuide/formatting.aspx)

### Design direction for Jobbers

- **Offer two single-column layouts.** Use **Classic** for reverse-chronological experience. Use **Projects First** for candidates whose strongest AI work is in projects, while keeping dated work history in an **Experience** section.
- **Keep styling controls focused.** Start with US Letter, an 11-point body font, bold 12-point section headings, black text, and roughly 0.7-inch margins. Offer a small font and spacing choice. Keep contact information in the main reading order, with standard headings and plain bullets. These are Jobbers defaults based on the research. [CareerOneStop formatting guide](https://cloudfront.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Resumes/ResumeGuide/formatting.aspx), [Greenhouse parsing guidance](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse)
- **Preview the export before saving.** Use the same layout for preview and PDF so candidates can check page breaks and keep section headings with their content. Verify that the exported text can be selected and copied in the expected order. [Reactive Resume template examples](https://rxresu.me/resume-templates)

## Tailor it to each posting

1. **Pick the three to five requirements that define the job.** Start with core responsibilities and required qualifications.
2. **Match each requirement to work you did.** Use the employer’s terminology when it accurately describes your experience. Move the strongest matching bullets to the top of each relevant job or project.
3. **Show what you built and what changed.** Include a verified result when available. Otherwise, describe the scope, users, or technical constraints. [CareerOneStop job-posting analysis](https://cloudfront.careeronestop.org/Veterans/JobSearch/ResumesAndApplications/analyzing-job-postings.aspx), [CareerOneStop work experience guide](https://cloudfront.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Resumes/ResumeGuide/work-experience.aspx)

## What to emphasize for the seven Jobbers roles

| Target role | Evidence to lead with |
| --- | --- |
| Forward Deployed Engineer | A customer deployment you owned, the systems you integrated, and what the customer adopted. |
| Product AI Engineer | An AI feature you shipped, who used it, and how you measured quality, cost, or latency. |
| Agentic AI Engineer | A multi-step workflow you built, how it used tools and handled failures, and how often it completed the task. |
| AI Evaluation Engineer | A benchmark or regression suite you designed and the release decision it informed. |
| Inference & AI Systems Engineer | A serving optimization with before-and-after throughput, latency, or GPU memory under comparable load. |
| Post-Training & Alignment Engineer | A fine-tuning or preference-training experiment with a baseline and held-out results. |
| AI Solutions Engineer / Architect | A customer problem you scoped, the architecture or prototype you delivered, and the decision it enabled. |

**Example for an evaluation role.** “Built a regression suite covering [N] representative queries, added it to CI, and caught [failure mode] before release.” Replace the placeholders with facts you can explain in an interview.
