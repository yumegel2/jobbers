# NYC Metro Cities and Neighborhoods

Use the **New York–Newark–Jersey City, NY–NJ Metropolitan Statistical Area (MSA 35620)** as a repeatable boundary for Jobbers. It covers 22 counties across New York and New Jersey. This reference uses the OMB delineation reflected in the BLS May 2025 definitions, checked November 21, 2026. [BLS area definitions](https://www.bls.gov/oes/2025/may/msa_def.htm), [OMB Bulletin 23-01](https://www.bls.gov/bls/omb-bulletin-23-01-revised-delineations-of-metropolitan-statistical-areas.pdf)

| Region | Counties in the metro |
| --- | --- |
| New York City | Bronx, Kings (Brooklyn), New York (Manhattan), Queens, Richmond (Staten Island) |
| Long Island suburbs | Nassau, Suffolk |
| Lower Hudson Valley | Putnam, Rockland, Westchester |
| New Jersey | Bergen, Essex, Hudson, Hunterdon, Middlesex, Monmouth, Morris, Ocean, Passaic, Somerset, Sussex, Union |

## Location matching notes for Jobbers

- Use a state, borough, county, ZIP, or address when a place name is ambiguous. Examples include Newark, NJ versus Newark, DE and Long Island City in Queens versus a broad Long Island listing. Use an employer’s precise location when available and label distances based on representative ZIP coordinates as approximate. [Census Gazetteer Files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/time-series/geo/gazetteer-files.2025.html)
- The wider combined statistical area extends into Connecticut and Pennsylvania. Stamford, CT, Poughkeepsie, NY, and Princeton, NJ fall outside MSA 35620 and are excluded by Jobbers. [BLS metro and combined-area definitions](https://www.bls.gov/ecec/factsheets/ecec-metro-areas-factsheet.htm), [BLS area definitions](https://www.bls.gov/oes/2025/may/msa_def.htm), [NJ county and municipality codes](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/pdf/current/1040i.pdf)
- Jobbers uses a local index of 2025 Census county subdivisions, plus selected neighborhood and postal aliases. When a name is ambiguous, the state or county helps resolve it. County-only postings can appear on the metro board, while ZIP-radius results require a location suitable for estimating distance. [Census Gazetteer Files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/time-series/geo/gazetteer-files.2025.html)
