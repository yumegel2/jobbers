# AI Job Requirements

Start with the role closest to work you can already demonstrate. Product engineering emphasizes shipped features, forward deployment emphasizes customer delivery, and specialist roles emphasize measurement, systems performance, or model training.

Based on employer postings reviewed November 21, 2026. Experience and education thresholds are specific to the cited postings. The examples in “What to show” are suggested ways to demonstrate the skills.

| Role | What employers ask for | What to show | Employer posting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) | OpenAI lists 5+ years in engineering or technical deployment with customer-facing experience. Build full-stack production software in Python, JavaScript, or similar, and show hands-on LLM deployment. This posting requires travel up to 50%. | A customer deployment you took from discovery to production, with your integration work and a measurable adoption or workflow outcome. | [OpenAI FDE](https://openai.com/careers/forward-deployed-engineer-%28fde%29-sf-san-francisco/) |
| AI Engineer (Product & Application) | Perplexity’s early-career posting asks for 1+ years of professional engineering or strong internships shipping production code. It calls for backend APIs and relational data modeling, React and TypeScript, and hands-on AI product experience. | A deployed retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) feature with a usable interface, a small evaluation set, and quality and latency measurements. | [Perplexity AI Products](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/Perplexity/daa9120e-94ff-46e0-b4bd-4d2d290cb409) |
| Agentic AI Engineer | LeanData’s staff posting asks for 4+ years building production systems, including 2+ shipping LLM-powered or agentic systems. It looks for orchestration, tool calling, structured outputs, retrieval, and context management. | A multi-step workflow with restricted tool permissions, recovery from failed calls, and tests for task completion. | [LeanData Agentic AI](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/leandata/9a371c01-13f4-4a6a-a9ad-91ede7ec54a4) |
| AI Evaluation Engineer | Anthropic looks for strong Python, production experience, and a record of designing, running, and analyzing model evaluations at scale. Statistics and experimental design strengthen an application. | A repeatable benchmark with a versioned dataset, a scoring rubric, and an analysis of a regression you found. | [Anthropic Model Evaluations](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5198255008) |
| Inference & AI Systems Engineer | OpenAI asks for systems programming, distributed infrastructure, and experience profiling and benchmarking. Model serving, GPU architecture, and CUDA or Triton are preferred in the cited posting. | A model-serving benchmark that reports before-and-after throughput, tail latency, and GPU memory under the same workload. | [OpenAI GPT Infrastructure](https://openai.com/careers/software-engineer-gpt-infrastructure-san-francisco/) |
| Post-Training & Alignment Engineer | Anthropic requires Python, deep learning frameworks, and distributed computing proficiency. It looks for hands-on LLM training, fine-tuning, or evaluation. It lists a relevant bachelor’s degree or equivalent education, training, or experience. | A reproducible fine-tuning experiment with a baseline, held-out evaluations, and analysis of where the tuned model improved or regressed. | [Anthropic Production Model Post-Training](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/4613592008) |
| AI Solutions Engineer / Architect | OpenAI looks for customer-facing technical experience, enterprise adoption, cloud architecture, data integration, identity and security, and the ability to prototype with APIs and explain tradeoffs. | A customer case study with discovery findings, an architecture diagram, a working proof of concept, and adoption or business success criteria. | [OpenAI Applied AI Architect](https://openai.com/careers/applied-ai-architect-san-francisco/) |

On your résumé, connect each example to what you owned, the constraint you worked within, and the outcome you measured. If you’re early in your career, the Perplexity posting gives an example of a path through internships and shipped product work.
