# How to Find AI Jobs Before They’re Posted

Follow the teams that might hire you and ask about upcoming headcount. Search for the work even when the job title varies.

| Roles | Early signals to watch |
| --- | --- |
| Forward Deployed Engineer, AI Solutions Engineer | Enterprise customer wins, new implementation partners, or a rollout into a regulated industry. |
| Product AI Engineer, Agentic AI Engineer | A new AI feature entering beta, a team launching integrations, or an engineering lead asking for design partners. |
| Evals, Inference, and Post-Training Engineers | A new model or serving stack, a benchmark release, or research authors sharing what their team is building next. |

- **Ask for warm introductions.** Give former coworkers, customers, or classmates a specific target. “Do you know anyone building out an AI deployment or evals team?”
- **Contact the team lead when you see a signal.** Mention what the team is building, share one relevant result, and ask whether they expect to hire in the next few months.
- **Become visible through relevant work.** Contribute a fix, benchmark, or integration to a project the team maintains. Share the result with the people working on it.

## Sample outreach

> Hi [Name], I saw your team is rolling out [specific project]. I worked on [similar problem] and achieved [result]. Are you planning to add anyone to the team in the next few months? I’d be happy to share a short write-up of the work.
