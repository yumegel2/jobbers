# What Applicants Check Before Applying

Applicants want to know what the job pays, what they would do, and where they would work. Employer identity and application effort also affect whether they proceed.

## What candidates look for

In iHire’s 2026 survey of 1,054 U.S. job seekers across 57 industries, these were the most requested details in job postings. Respondents could select multiple answers. [iHire survey, Figures 26–27 and methodology](https://www.ihirelegal.com/resourcecenter/employer/pages/the-state-of-online-recruiting-2026)

| Information | Share | What to show in Jobbers |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Salary range | 75.4% | Employer-provided range, currency, and pay period. Label base pay, bonus, and equity when supplied. |
| Detailed responsibilities | 63.9% | What the person would own and do day to day. Include required and preferred qualifications as the employer labels them. |
| Work arrangement | 56.3% | On-site, hybrid, or remote, plus the listed office location and any commute, schedule, or travel requirements. |
| Benefits | 47.9% | Employer-provided benefits such as health coverage, paid leave, and retirement. |
| Hiring contact | 45.7% | Recruiter or hiring contact when the employer publishes it. |

Built In’s 2024 candidate research also put salary (71%), benefits (64%), and work-life balance (64%) among the most important information candidates seek when researching an employer. [Built In Candidate Preferences report](https://employers.builtin.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Built-In_-2024-Candidate-Preferences.pdf)

## What can stop an application

In iHire’s survey, 60.7% said missing salary information would make them less likely to apply, 47.9% were deterred by postings that omit the employer name, and 41.3% cited applications that take more than 10 minutes. These are reported preferences from a cross-industry sample. [iHire survey, Figure 27](https://www.ihirelegal.com/resourcecenter/employer/pages/the-state-of-online-recruiting-2026)

## Implications for Jobbers

Put company, title, NYC metro location, work arrangement, and pay near the top. Use **View details** for the full employer description, qualifications, benefits, and any published application instructions. Keep the original posting link visible, and label missing fields as **Not provided** wherever the page reserves a spot for them. See [Jobbers features](./Jobbers_Features.md) for the listing content requirement.
